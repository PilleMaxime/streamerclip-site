Twitch → TikTok

Highlight clips, without the editing.

StreamerClip watches a Twitch chat while it's live, catches the moments that actually landed, and turns them into short vertical clips ready for TikTok.

What it does

StreamerClip runs continuously against the operator's own Twitch channels. When chat activity spikes around a keyword, an emote, or a sudden burst of messages, it cuts a short vertical clip around that moment, crops the webcam and gameplay automatically, and adds karaoke-style captions.

How publishing works

  1. 01
    Review

    Every clip is previewed by the operator before it goes anywhere — nothing is posted sight unseen.

  2. 02
    Connect

    A TikTok account is connected once via TikTok Login Kit, showing the operator exactly which account and which privacy options are available.

  3. 03
    Publish

    An approved clip is uploaded to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft, for the operator to finalize from the TikTok app.

Who runs it

StreamerClip is operated for use with TikTok accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage. It isn't a public sign-up product — see the Terms of Service for details.
