What StreamerClip is

StreamerClip is an automation tool that watches Twitch livestreams, detects highlight moments from chat activity, and prepares short vertical clips for TikTok. It is operated for use with TikTok accounts the operator owns or is authorized to manage — it is not offered as a public sign-up service.

Connecting a TikTok account

By connecting a TikTok account through TikTok Login Kit, you confirm that you own that account, or that its owner has authorized you to manage its content on their behalf. Each clip is reviewed and approved individually by the account owner before it reaches TikTok — nothing is posted automatically without that review.

Acceptable use

StreamerClip may only be used to publish content the account owner has the rights to share, and in line with TikTok's own Community Guidelines and Terms of Service. Access may be withdrawn at any time if a connected account is used in a way that violates either.

Revoking access

A connected account can be disconnected at any time from TikTok's own settings — Settings → Manage account → Apps and websites → StreamerClip. This immediately revokes StreamerClip's access to that account.
