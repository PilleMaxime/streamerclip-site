Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 August 2026

Scope

This policy covers the information StreamerClip accesses when a TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, and how that information is used.

Information accessed

  • Basic profile information — username, display name, and avatar (user.info.basic) — read-only, used to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload video content and to check which posting and privacy options are available for that account (video.upload, video.publish), used only at the moment a clip is being published.

How it's used

Profile information is shown inside StreamerClip so the operator can confirm which account a clip is about to be published to, and to choose the correct visibility for that clip. Video is uploaded to TikTok only after a specific clip has been manually reviewed and approved — never automatically, and never in bulk.

What isn't collected

StreamerClip does not read TikTok direct messages, does not access followers or following lists, and does not collect any data from people who later view a published clip. Clip content itself comes entirely from the connected Twitch stream, never from TikTok.

Storage & sharing

Access tokens are stored on the server that runs StreamerClip and are never shared with, sold to, or used by any third party. They exist solely to keep a TikTok account connected between sessions.

Retention & revocation

Tokens are kept only while an account stays connected. Disconnecting StreamerClip from TikTok's own settings — Settings → Manage account → Apps and websites — deletes StreamerClip's access immediately; the operator also removes the corresponding stored token.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above reflects the latest revision.
